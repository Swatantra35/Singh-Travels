About Singh Travels

Rooted in Varanasi, Driven by Commitment

Singh Travels was founded in 1986 with a commitment to providing safe, comfortable, and reliable journeys to pilgrims and travellers in Varanasi. From local temple tours to long-distance outstation journeys, we ensure every ride is smooth and memorable.

  • Experienced & Verified Drivers Background-checked chauffeurs who know every lane and ghat of Kashi.
  • Transparent Tour Rates Clear upfront pricing with zero hidden charges. What you see is what you pay.
  • Punctual 24/7 Pickup On-time pickup from Babatpur Airport, Varanasi Junction & hotels for early morning Ganga Aarti.

Experience the ancient soul, sacred rituals, and divine atmosphere of the holiest city on Earth.

Swarved Mahamandir (Sarnath)

The famous 7-floor carved white marble meditation temple & spiritual center in Varanasi.

Namo Ghat (Varanasi)

The modern iconic riverfront ghat famous for its majestic Namaste sculptures & Ganges view.
